# Licence for data and media

The code and original text of this project are dedicated to the public domain under the Unlicense (see `UNLICENSE`).

The **data and media** (the roster, the place, person, unit and source records, transcriptions, exports, and our own
photographs and figures) are **also** dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)**:
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. CC0 is more widely recognised for data, and it includes a
fallback licence for places where a public-domain dedication is not valid.

## What this does not cover

Third-party material keeps its own status. Every image and document record carries `rights` and `credit` fields
saying what applies to it. Images used by written permission are marked "not public domain", are left out of the
bulk downloads, and may not be reused without asking their holder.

The underlying historical records (pension files, registers, manuscripts) are cited, not relicensed: their own
public-domain status is recorded per copy on the approved-sources list.
