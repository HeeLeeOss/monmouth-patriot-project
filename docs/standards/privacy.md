# Privacy

The project's subjects are people who died long ago. Their descendants, the people who research them, and the people
who contribute to this project are not subjects.

## The rules

- **No living person** appears in the project's content, data or exports **without their written consent**. This
  covers descendants, lineage-society applicants and members, researchers, contributors, and anyone named in modern
  paperwork. Consent is stored in `research/_permissions/` and can be withdrawn.
- **Lineage stops before 1950.** A line of descent from a patriot is followed only through people who died before
  1950, unless every living person it would reveal has given written consent.
- **Modern records stay out.** Lineage-society applications, membership records, and modern genealogical submissions
  are not sources of personal data, even where they are public.
- **Contributors choose their own attribution.** We record only the name or handle a contributor chooses to give, and
  nothing else about them.
- **Location stays on the device.** Any "near me" feature uses the browser's location on the visitor's device only and
  never sends it anywhere.
- **No tracking.** No cookies for analytics, no advertising trackers.

## When in doubt

Leave it out and ask. A person can be added later with consent; a published name cannot be fully withdrawn.
